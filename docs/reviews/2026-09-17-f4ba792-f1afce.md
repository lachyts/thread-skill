## Disposition (2026-09-17, lead session event-script-backlog-orient)

stopped this time**: its two tasks are `cross-cutting` and `single-file` (so the read-only defect in
finding 4 cannot fire), both are explicitly `model: opus` (finding 5 needs an unrecognised tier), and
no `judgeModel` is passed (finding 2). Finding 1 could fire but costs framing, not capability, and
