## What survives — the design, which the review did not dispute

Ruled by Lachy in the grill of 2026-09-19, and still correct:

- He tracks a handoff as a **single object**: he either acts on it, or converts it into a
  task himself. A close that proposes tasks duplicating it is a second tracker for work he
  is already tracking.
- Trigger on **the doc existing**, not on who asked for it — a hook-forced handoff counts.
- **Scope is the line**: only the thread's *continuation* is suppressed; a loose end
  unrelated to the thread still reaches the menu.
- **No annotation, no asking** whether he has done it.
