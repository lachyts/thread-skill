# 0065 — A landing takes the checker's verdicts

Date: 2026-11-02
Status: accepted. Builds fresh-review's ADR 0001 (`~/.agents/skills/fresh-review/docs/adr/0001-confirmed-findings-end-chains.md`) into close's landing loop, land.sh, a Leg's review and the weekly reading. Lachy ruled ADR 0001 on 2026-10-31: a finding blocks only when a separate check reproduces it, the change introduced or worsened it, and it meets the blocking test, with an error-path loss counting only on an ordinary trigger or one the change causes; a chain has three code rounds; a confirmed finding still open at the budget is fixed until its reproduction passes, with no further review. The technical calls below were decided for him in fresh-review's Phase 1 plan and in this task (fresh-review p1-4). Supersedes in part ADR 0058: Decision 4's four-round budget and its ledger part, and its Consequences' answer to the self-grading risk. Amends ADR 0028 Decision 3 a second time.

## Context

ADR 0058 left the grading with the session that wrote the code, and kept a four-round budget. The estate's record since says neither held: no Claude round has come back empty (0 of 452 since 2026-09-15), 67 of 113 logged rounds hit the cap of 15, 64 of 223 graded findings were repeats, and event-scanner's failover landing was still blocking at the budget twice, each round finding a fresh rare-trigger loss in the previous round's fixes, and went to Lachy both times. fresh-review's ADR 0001 answers it in that skill: a **checker**, a second fresh agent, gives every finding a verdict (`blocking`, `unconfirmed`, `filed`, `rejected`) and writes a failing test for each `blocking` one; the review doc carries `round:`, `base:` and `tree:`; `review-ledger.py` no longer stops anything; `review-round.py` records `unconfirmed` and no `verdict`.

Close's loop, land.sh's reviewed-commit guard, a Leg's review and the weekly reading all still followed ADR 0058. Landed alone, fresh-review's new text would contradict them, so Phase 1 lands together: this ADR's change first, then fresh-review's.

## Decision

1. **Close takes the checker's verdicts and grades nothing.** Loop step 5 is *Confirm*: fresh-review's Step 3.5 checks the round, and its `## Confirm` table is the round's grade. The session grades nothing. A verdict close believes wrong is said in `## Known deliberate` and stands. A `blocking` reproduction that close believes shows intended behaviour is a product call, a decision for Lachy. `unconfirmed` findings are filed with the `filed` ones, in the landing's follow-up task. A round whose checker is lost twice is **unconfirmed**: it is never clean, and the landing holds.
2. **Three code rounds per landing, numbered in fresh-review's `round:`.** The landing's chain is its own: its first round names no round before (`round: 1`), whatever a dev-time chain on the branch reached, since every landing round reviews the whole branch afresh. A resumed loop takes the round before from the landing's own docs: the highest `round:` among the code docs added on the branch since its PR opened. That is ADR 0058's `createdAt` window, now reading `round:` instead of counting the docs it adds, so a session ended mid-loop, a stuck plain call or a hold never restarts the count.
3. **Fix until green at the budget.** A blocking finding on the third round is fixed until its reproduction passes and the Verify line is green, then committed with its reproduction, and nothing reviews it again. Only a fix that cannot be made to pass, or one that needs a product or design call, reaches Lachy, as `Blocking, open:` with its reproduction. A third round with no blocking finding is clean at any count. Every round still reviews the whole branch.
4. **land.sh `--fixed-at-budget`.** O10 refused `--queue` when any commit after `--reviewed` touched more than `docs/reviews/`, so the budget's fixes could never queue. With the flag, a code commit after the reviewed commit R is accepted when it changes a path that a `blocking` row of the budget round's `## Confirm` table names, matched whole: the fix committed with its reproduction, or, for prose, the file the quoted lines are in. The doc is found in R..HEAD's history: the review doc whose latest version says `mode: code`, `round:` 3 or more and a `head:` R starts with, read from before close's deletion commit. Once a fix follows R, R's checks no longer speak for HEAD's code, so only HEAD's are read. Without the flag, or with no code commit after R, O10 is unchanged.
5. **The ledger is a churn report.** Close's loop step 8 (*Ledger*) and its `review ledger failed` hold are gone; step 8 is *Next*. score.py still reads the ledger's ORIGINAL / REGRESSION split for review churn.
6. **No round is left after the budget, so CI and a late red Verify hold.** A `ci failed`, or a red Verify run after a clean round, once the chain has had its three rounds has no round to review its fix and no reproduction to tie it to under `--fixed-at-budget`. It is a decision for Lachy. Before the budget it goes on as before: fix, Verify, next round.
7. **A Leg takes the checker's verdicts too.** A Leg's one round runs Step 3.5 as written, with the task's Verify line as the named test, and judges nothing itself. Each reproduction the checker kept is committed with its fix in a `Task:` commit, or removed when the task is set aside, so none rides into a later task's commits. Each task's round is a chain of its own, `round: 1`. Execute's `max_review_rounds` (4) stays: it caps the engine's master-review rounds, which is a separate loop from close's landing chain and from a Leg's single round.
8. **The readers follow.** The attention ledger's Review trails retire `simplifyRounds`, `stopRounds`, `stops`, `stopsHanded` and `handedAfterStop`, because no landing runs a simplify round or meets a ledger STOP any more; older `attention` lines still read. The weekly `freshReview` entry reports each landing's `unconfirmed`, `checkedRounds` and `confirmRate` (reproduced over candidates), and span's `freshReviewLevels` reports precision per mode and level: the share of findings the checker did not reject. The weekly reading's self-grading check becomes the **confirm-rate check**: a week whose checked rounds held two or more candidates and reproduced none of them is named.

## Considered options

- **Key `--fixed-at-budget` off the Disposition's `Blocking, fixed:` rows.** Rejected: the reproduction there sits in free text inside parentheses, which `review-round.py` deliberately never counts, and a held landing's doc says `Blocking, open:`. The `## Confirm` table's verdict cell is structured, and it is the checker's, not the session's.
- **Accept any commit after R once a round-3 doc exists.** Rejected: it keeps the budget but drops the guard. A filed finding's fix, a tidy or a CI fix would ride in unreviewed.
- **Count a dev-time chain into the landing's.** Rejected: a session that ran three dev-time rounds and then kept committing would reach close with no round left and no reviewed head to queue, and a session ended mid-loop would leave its successor no way to find the round before but by recency, which fresh-review forbids.
- **A fourth round to review a CI fix after the budget.** Rejected: fresh-review dispatches no fourth (its Step 2, check 3), and ADR 0001 ends the chain at three.
- **Change banner B**, whose "any own-branch review ran clean here" reads loosely after the budget's fixes. Not now: the Chorus Stage pins the sentence in its own tests, so it is a cross-repo change. The landing row says `fixed at the budget`.

## Consequences

- **The session grades nothing,** so ADR 0058's bias risk is gone. The risk that replaces it is a lenient checker, read weekly through its confirm rate.
- **A landing can merge commits no review read:** the budget's fixes, each with its passing reproduction and a green Verify run. That is ADR 0001's accepted cost, and land.sh holds it to exactly those commits.
- **ADR 0028 Decision 3's budget** is now three code rounds, ending on confirmed findings.
- **A held landing resumed at the budget** needs no further round: once Lachy's call settles the finding, it is fixed until its reproduction passes and queued with `--fixed-at-budget`, which reads the doc from history.
- **Verify:** thread-skill's `make test` (tests/land.test.sh case 77, the contract tests `close-lands-own-branch`, `execute-legs`, `reflect-attention` and `reflect-weekly`) passes, and the weekly Reflection's span runs on `review-round` lines that carry `unconfirmed` and no `verdict`.
